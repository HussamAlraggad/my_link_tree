# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers, freelance clients, peers in the dev and design community, and anyone who receives Hussam's link in a bio, a message, or on a card. They are almost always on a phone, often on mobile data, and decide in seconds whether to reach out.

## Product Purpose

A one-page hub for Hussam Alraggad's public channels. It should load instantly, make the next action obvious, and feel unmistakably like him: a software engineer with a sense of humor. Success means the visitor emails, follows, or opens a profile without waiting for anything to load.

## Positioning

Software engineering first, with frontend craft and AI leverage. The page itself is proof of craft: a tiny, fast, accessible page built by hand, not a template with a logo dropped into it.

## Operating Context

- Opened from social bios, messages, QR codes, and a phone home screen.
- Often on a slow or metered connection; first paint must be near-instant.
- The visitor arrives knowing little or nothing; the page must explain who he is in one line.
- Deployed as a static GitHub Pages project site at `hussamalraggad.github.io/my_link_tree/`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- HTML and CSS only, plus a JSON-LD metadata block. No build step, framework, package manager, CDN, or executable JavaScript.
- Total first-load weight under 100 KB, including type and the mark.
- Links work without JavaScript; there is no client-side behavior code.
- Six confirmed channels: Email, GitHub, LinkedIn, Dribbble, Behance, Instagram.
- The supplied character-face logo is the identity mark. No photo.
- No analytics, cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party requests.
- No invented employers, clients, metrics, testimonials, or credentials.

## Brand Commitments

- Public identity: Hussam Alraggad, AI software engineer.
- The character-face mark (round glasses, large nose, handlebar mustache) is the enduring brand asset, supplied in black, colored, and white.
- The link page uses the Contact Sheet world: warm paper, ink, mark red, the colored character mark, and very faint black-mark watermarks. The portfolio's Runtime system remains the portfolio's world.
- Voice: direct, precise, quietly funny. No hype, no buzzword stacks.

## Evidence on Hand

- Logo source files in `logo/` (black, colored, white), 500x500 with transparency.
- Links and handles confirmed from `portfolio_04`: GitHub `@HussamAlraggad`, LinkedIn `in/hussam-al-raggad`, Dribbble `@HussamAlraggad`, Behance `@HussamAlraggad`, Instagram `@hussam.alraggad.designs`, email `hussamraedalraggad@gmail.com`.
- No testimonials, client logos, press, or published metrics. None should be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. Speed is part of the brand.
2. One screen, one decision: reach me.
3. The mark carries the personality; the interface stays precise.
4. Accessible by default: keyboard, contrast, reduced motion, no-JS.
5. Nothing that cannot be maintained by hand in two files and a few assets.
